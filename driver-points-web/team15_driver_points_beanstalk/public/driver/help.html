Driver Resources

Welcome to the Driver Help Center. Here you'll find answers to common questions about how points are earned, redeemed, and managed in the Driver Points program.

How Points Are Earned

🚚

Completed Deliveries

Points are awarded by your sponsor for each completed delivery or trip based on their point rate.

⭐

Performance Bonuses

Sponsors may award bonus points for exceptional performance, on-time delivery, or high ratings.

📅

Sponsor Adjustments

Your sponsor can manually add or deduct points at their discretion, visible in your Point History.

🏆

Milestones & Promotions

Special milestone events or sponsor promotions may grant extra points during limited periods.

Frequently Asked Questions

Points are awarded by your sponsor organization based on your activity (deliveries, performance, etc.). The exact criteria and rate are set by each sponsor. Visit your Dashboard to see your current balance and Point History for a detailed breakdown.
Each sponsor maintains a separate points account for you. Points earned under one sponsor cannot be combined with or transferred to another sponsor's account. Use the "Active Sponsor" selector on your Dashboard to switch between accounts and view each balance.
Go to the Catalog page to browse available reward items offered by your active sponsor. Add items to your cart and submit an order. Points will be deducted from your balance once the order is confirmed.
Each sponsor sets their own points-to-dollar conversion rate (in cents per point). Your current sponsor's conversion rate is displayed on your Dashboard under "Points to Dollar Conversion." For example, if the rate is 1 cent per point, then 100 points = $1.00.
Points can decrease if your sponsor manually adjusts your balance (deduction) or if you redeem points for a catalog item. Check your Point History on the Dashboard to see each transaction, including the reason and who made the adjustment.
On your Dashboard, scroll to the "Find a Sponsor" section. Search for a sponsor organization by name and click "Apply." You can submit an optional message with your application. Your application status will appear in "My Sponsor Applications."
Yes. In the "My Sponsor Applications" section of your Dashboard, any application with a PENDING status will show a "Withdraw" button. Clicking it will cancel that application.
If your membership with a sponsor is terminated, you may lose access to that sponsor's catalog and redemption options. Your earned points history is still recorded, but unredeemed points may no longer be usable depending on the sponsor's policy. Contact your sponsor for more information.
Your submitted orders are listed in the "My Orders" section on your Dashboard. Each order shows its current status: PENDING, PROCESSING, COMPLETED, or CANCELLED. Click "Refresh" to get the latest updates.
Point adjustments are made directly by your sponsor. If you believe a point adjustment was made in error, please reach out to your sponsor organization's representative. Your Point History on the Dashboard shows who made each manual adjustment under the "Adjusted By" column.
